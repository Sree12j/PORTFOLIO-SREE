01 — About

Part teacher, part tinkerer.

Aluva, Kerala

I like the moment a hard idea finally clicks — for a student in my classroom, and for me when something I've been learning starts to work.

I'm an Assistant Professor who teaches Python, databases, web technologies and computer networks, and a developer who keeps picking up new things — most recently AI, machine learning and Google's agent tooling. I enjoy explaining technical concepts in plain language, and I care about knowledge that can be put to practical use.

I'm building my career in IT with one foot in education and the other in technology and innovation, because I think the best way to understand something is to build it, and the best way to master it is to teach it.
